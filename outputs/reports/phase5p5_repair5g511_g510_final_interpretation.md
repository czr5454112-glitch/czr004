# Phase5.5 Repair5G.5.11 G5.10 Final Interpretation

G5.10 was a good engineering and scientific result, not a learned-runtime success. It made the G5.9 bounded goal-aware dual-channel UpdateLTM parameter lattice executable in the project-owned C++ harness and passed adapter parity on static, additive, static-abstain, and C-only/F-disabled paths.

The only G5.10 blocker was scale: the local slice measured 2 contexts, so confidence targets, feature signal, policy training, Phase5.5, Phase6, runtime learned-policy, and AAAI-ready claims all stayed closed.

G5.11 therefore treats G5.10 as an executable adapter and runs the full observed-ID server lattice. It does not change PIBT, LaCAM*, candidate generation, pruning, restarts, h-values, candidate deletion, or `external/lacam2/lacam2/**`.

The interpretation after G5.11 is sharper:

```text
The bounded lattice oracle upper bound is real on the 60-context observed bank.
The label distribution is still not safe enough for a learned policy.
```

So the next research step is label/coverage design for static, abstention, no-solution, and longer-budget cases, not policy training.
